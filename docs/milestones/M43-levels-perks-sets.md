# M43 Levels, perks and sets

Status: planned.

## Goal

A character grows: kills and discoveries give XP, each level gives a point for one of three perk trees, and gear sets grant perks of their own. Builds differ between players and every boss raises the ceiling.

## Depends on

M23 (items, character file, RPC pattern for per player data). File level detail was written against the code of 2026-11-16 (M23 built, `Net.PROTOCOL_VERSION` 32); where a name below no longer exists, the code is right.

## Design

- `Perk` (resource): id, name, text, a list of stat modifiers (`stat`, `add`, `mul`) and optional flags for effects the sim checks by name (for example `parry_stagger_long`, `roll_cheap`, `bolt_pierce`). Perks come from three sources and stack through one path: the trees, single items (`ItemDef.perks`) and full sets (`ItemDef.set`, `SetDef` with the pieces needed and its perks).
- `Stats`: one block per player (max health and stamina, regen, damage per weapon type, defense, roll cost, carry weight, gather speed, flags). The server recomputes it when level, perks or gear change and sends it to the owner by reliable RPC; `SimPlayer` and `Combat` read `Stats` instead of raw `Tuning` values on both sides. Not rolled back: it changes rarely, and a few ticks of mismatch correct themselves.
- XP: kills, first discovery of a camp, site or realm, bosses. The curve and all amounts are data.
- Group sharing: a kill gives its full XP to every living player within 40 m and to everyone who damaged the enemy, wherever they stand. It is not split, so playing together never pays less than playing alone. Dead players get none. Discoveries and bosses pay everyone in the realm.
- Death drops XP (the user's idea, 2026-11-14, like Dark Souls and Elden Ring): the XP gained toward the next level falls into the grave M23 leaves at the place of death, next to the bag. Touching the grave gives it back. Dying again before that replaces the grave's XP with the new, smaller amount: the old XP is lost. Levels already reached and perk points are never lost. The HUD shows how much lies in the grave and where it is.
- Level cap: 10 per fallen boss plus 10 (`World.flags`), so levels cannot outrun the realm order. The character keeps its level in a world with fewer flags; only gaining is capped.
- Trees (data, `game/data/perks/`): warrior (sword, shield, parry), ranger (crossbow, movement, stamina), crafter (gathering, carrying, crafting, building). About 10 perks each in tier 1, some with prerequisites; tiers 2 and 3 arrive with M45 and M46. Respec at the arrival gate for a cost (the keep is gone, M34).

## Code as it is (what the tasks hook into)

- Numbers a player's sim reads today: `Tuning.data()` (`data/tuning.tres`, `sim/tuning.gd`): `max_health` (set in `SimPlayer.respawn`, line ~650), `max_stamina` and `stamina_regen` (stamina tick, ~376), `roll_stamina` (~408), `sprint_stamina` (~278), `block_stamina` (~539), `bolt_stamina_min/max` (~599), `walk_speed` and `sprint_speed` (~279), `block_reduction` and `block_cap` (`Loadout.block_share`). Damage dealt: `AttackDef.damage * Loadout.damage_scale(main_hand)` in `SimPlayer._strike`, bolts `lerpf(bolt_damage_min, bolt_damage_max, charge) * Loadout.damage_scale(ranged)` in `_shoot`. Damage taken: `Loadout.damage_taken(def.damage, armor)` in `SimPlayer._take`. These are the places `Stats` replaces `Tuning` reads. Tests that call `SimPlayer.step` directly must keep their numbers: `Stats` defaults equal the `Tuning` values (the same trick as the `GEAR` defaults).
- `Loadout` (`sim/loadout.gd`) is already the "gear reads into a few ints" path: `Loadout.of(inventory)` and `Inventories._loadout_ints`/`_apply_gear` (runs in `NetworkRollback.after_process_tick`, idempotent). `Stats` follows it: a pure `Stats.of(level, perks, inventory)` on the server, applied to the `SimPlayer` after every rollback step. The block prediction needs is small (max health, max stamina, stamina regen, roll cost, damage multipliers, speed, flags as a bitmask) and is NOT rolled back: the server sends it by RPC and the owner's `SimPlayer` gets the same block at once; other players' blocks travel with the same RPC to everyone (it changes rarely).
- Item data: `ItemDef` (`sim/item_def.gd`, `data/items/<id>.tres`; `Items` registry in `sim/items.gd`, append only `IDS`) has `stamina` and the other stats as data only (trinket and armor `stamina` are unused until this milestone); add `perks: Array[Perk]` and `set_id: StringName` there. `Inventory` (`sim/inventory.gd`) has the worn cells (`Inventory.Cell.*`); a set counts worn pieces by `set_id`.
- Kills: `Spawner._tend` sees the first tick a member is `DOWN` and pays loot once through `_looted`; the boss is `Encounter._rollback_tick` (`Spawner._loot_bosses`, `boss_looted`). XP uses the same once-only memory (`_xp_paid`, not rolled back, reset when the camp reopens). Who damaged an enemy is NOT recorded yet: `Enemy.take_hit(amount, from, knockback, flinch)` has no attacker. Add a server-memory `Enemy.damagers` (peer ids, written by `SimPlayer._strike` and `_fly_bolts`, never read inside rollback, cleared by `wake`/`become`/`reset`; a replayed tick writes the same ids) or a `Spawner`-side dictionary filled from the player's `swing_hits`. Training dummies are enemy indices 0 and 1: no XP.
- Death and graves: `Graves._watch` (`net/graves.gd`) buries the bag at the first DEAD step of a `respawns` value (`_buried`); `Looting.bury(owner_id, bag, position, up)` makes the GRAVE `WorldObjects` entry and `Looting.open` (key E) gives the stack back; `WorldObjects.to_dict`/`from_dict` persist graves with clamped fields. The XP in a grave is one more field of that entry (`xp`), paid out by `Looting.open`; a second death makes a second grave and clears `xp` of the owner's older grave ("the old XP is lost").
- Character file: `CharacterSave` (`client/character_save.gd`, `make`/`write`/`read`, `VERSION` 1, fields `id`, `name`, `inventory`, `junk`); `GameServer._send_character` (at most every `CHARACTER_EVERY` seconds, dirty flag `_character_dirty`) sends it, the client writes it; `GameServer._on_hello` reads it and clamps it. Level, XP and perks go into the same dictionary (`level`, `xp`, `perks` as a list of ids; unknown ids dropped and the points re-derived: `points = level - 1 - spent`, so a hand edit cannot buy perks). `VERSION` stays 1 (missing fields read as level 1, no perks) unless a field changes meaning.
- RPC pattern for per player data: `Net` (`net/net.gd`) has `inventory_snapshot`/`inventory_changed` (server to one client) and `inventory_intent(op, a, b)` (client to server, 30 a second in `Inventories`); `GameClient` keeps `inventory` and emits signals the panel reads. Add `Net.stats`, `Net.progress` (level, xp, points, perks) and an intent `Net.perk_intent(op, id)` (spend, respec); bump `Net.PROTOCOL_VERSION` (32 now). The XP toast follows `Net.item_toast`.
- World flags: `World.flags()` (names of cleared bosses) gives the level cap, `10 + 10 * flags.size()`; checked on gain only.
- UI: `InventoryPanel` (I or Tab) is the pattern for a panel with intents; the character panel (task 4) is its own `CharacterPanel` on key C (`Controls`, `project.godot` input map), built with `Menus` helpers; `ItemTooltip.rows()` is the data of a tooltip (add set rows there); `Hud` has `notice(text)` toasts and the health and stamina bars (read `Stats` maxima instead of `Tuning`). Level-up sound: add a name to `Sounds.library` (synthesize in `tools/make_sounds.py`), glow on `PlayerView`.
- Tests to copy: `tests/inventory_test.gd` (server side inventory rules), `tests/loot_test.gd` (spawner kills and drops, `FlatGround`), `tests/save_test.gd` (graves, character and world files), `tests/combat_test.gd` (numbers: rerun after `Stats`), the headless network test in `tools/check.py` (bots, lag).

## Out of scope

Skills that rise by use. Classes. Perks for realms that do not exist yet.

## Pitfalls

- `Stats` feeds prediction: the owner must have the same block as the server before the tick that uses it, or every swing is corrected. Send on change, apply at once on both sides, and test it under lag.
- Flags are checked by name in sim code: keep them few and each with a test, or they rot.
- Balance by data only; no perk may need a new rolled back field without a wire serializer and a protocol bump.
